Pork Adobo

Description

Pork adobo is a classic Filipino comfort food made by braising pork belly or shoulder in a savory, tangy reduction of soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and whole black peppercorns.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs pork belly or pork shoulder, cut into 1.5-inch cubes
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup cane vinegar or coconut vinegar
  • 6 cloves garlic, crushed or chopped
  • 1-2 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp whole black peppercorns
  • 1/2 to 1 cup water
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional, to balance tartness)

Steps

  1. Brown the meat: In a large pot or pan, sear the pork pieces over medium heat until lightly browned on all sides. The pork will render its own fat.
  2. Aromatics: Add the crushed garlic and whole peppercorns, sautéing for about a minute until fragrant.
  3. Simmer: Pour in the soy sauce, vinegar, water, and bay leaves. Do not stir immediately after adding the vinegar. Bring the mixture to a boil, then lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 45 minutes to 1 hour until the pork is tender.
  4. Reduce: Remove the lid and let the sauce reduce until it thickens into a glossy glaze over the pork.
  5. Serve: Serve hot with a generous mound of white rice.
